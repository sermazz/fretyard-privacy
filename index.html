Privacy Policy

Fretyard for Android (Google Play testing build) · Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: Fretyard is an offline-first music learning, practice, and songwriting app. In the current Google Play testing build, your songs, exercises, settings, and saved audio notes are stored on your device, not on a Fretyard server. Google Play handles in-app purchases.

1. Who operates Fretyard

Fretyard is published by an independent developer under the name Fretyard (Android package name: com.fretyard.app).

For privacy questions or requests, contact fretyardstudio@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The app may store information that you create or configure, including:

  • Saved songs, chord progressions, musical notes, and notebook entries.
  • Lesson progress, practice results, and time spent practicing.
  • Instrument settings and other app preferences.
  • Audio notes that you explicitly choose to record and save.

This information is stored in the app's local device storage. The current testing build does not provide Fretyard accounts or upload this content to Fretyard-operated servers.

3. Microphone access and recordings

Fretyard requests microphone permission when you activate a feature that needs it, such as tuning, pitch detection, or recording an audio note. Pitch and audio analysis are performed on your device. Recordings are saved locally only when you choose to save them.

Fretyard does not transmit microphone audio to a Fretyard server in this testing build. You can refuse or revoke microphone permission through your Android settings; features requiring the microphone will then be unavailable.

4. Purchases through Google Play

Fretyard offers optional Beginner and Advanced purchases through Google Play Billing. Google processes the purchase and associated Google account, payment, and transaction information under its own privacy policies. Fretyard asks Google Play which eligible products you own and uses the result to unlock the corresponding features.

Fretyard does not receive your payment-card details, and this testing build does not send purchase tokens to a Fretyard backend. For more about Google's handling of information, see the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Exports and sharing

You can choose to export or share material from the app, including backups and audio notes. When you do, the files or information are sent to the destination or app you select, such as a file manager, messaging app, or cloud storage provider. That destination may handle the information according to its own privacy policy. Fretyard does not automatically send your notebook or recordings to other users.

6. Analytics, advertising, and other services

In this Android testing build, Fretyard does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or a Fretyard-operated account or cloud-backup service. Network access may be used for Google Play purchase checks and other services provided by your device or operating system.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may back up some app data under your device and Google account settings. This is separate from a Fretyard-operated backup service.

This privacy-policy webpage contains no analytics scripts or advertising. If hosted using GitHub Pages, GitHub may process ordinary connection information when you visit the page, under its own privacy statement.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your locally stored content remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. You can remove local content through Fretyard's settings, including Settings → Data and backups → Delete all local data. You may want to export a backup first. Deleting local data does not automatically delete files you previously shared or backups held by other services.

Purchase and payment records maintained by Google are managed according to Google's policies and your Google Play account settings. Android or device backups may have separate retention rules.

If you contact the developer by email, your message and email address will be used to respond to your request and may be retained as needed to manage the correspondence and comply with applicable obligations. You may request deletion of that correspondence when applicable.

8. Security

Fretyard relies on the operating system's application storage and permission controls to help protect locally stored information. No method of digital storage is completely secure. We recommend keeping your device updated and protecting exported backups appropriately.

9. Questions and privacy rights

For questions about this policy or to exercise rights available under applicable privacy law, contact fretyardstudio@gmail.com. Because the current app has no Fretyard account or central database of your musical content, the developer generally cannot access or remotely delete work saved only on your device.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current Fretyard Android Google Play testing build. If future versions add cloud accounts, server-side purchase verification, advertising, analytics, or other data processing, this policy will be updated before those features are introduced. The updated date at the top will show when changes were made.
